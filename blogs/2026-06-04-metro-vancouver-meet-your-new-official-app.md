Transit has been named TransLink's recommended trip-planning app for Metro Vancouver for the next five years, giving 300,000+ monthly riders access to Transit Royale with unlimited departure schedules and additional trip planning options. The partnership includes automatic detour detection that spots over 200 bus detours monthly, real-time rider feedback via Rate-My-Ride, and special FIFA World Cup 2026 Vancouver features like event locations and custom routing to BC Place.
